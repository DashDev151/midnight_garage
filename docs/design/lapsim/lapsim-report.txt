# RAN WHEN PARKED - lap-time model, all cars (external review, prototype)

## The model
Lap time = quasi-static point-mass sim over a course of corners + straights.
 - corner apex speed = sqrt(mu * g * radius), mu from the signed-off grip model
 - straights: accelerate under wheel power (the lesser of power-limited and the drivetrain
   traction limit), aero drag = Cd x frontal area (0.82 x width x height), top speed
   capped at the published figure, then grip-limited braking into the next corner
 - torque delivery shapes corner-exit pull; a transition/agility term costs heavy,
   low-grip cars time in tight corners. 0-100 validated against published figures.
 - PROTOTYPE: drivetrain-launch and agility constants are first-pass; frontal areas are
   REAL (0.82 x width x height) for all 85 cars from published dimensions.

## The four courses
- Touge: 1.5 km, 8 corners (3 hairpin, 3 slow, 2 medium), 59% straight, avg radius 56 m, longest straight 180 m
- Mountain: 3.4 km, 10 corners (3 slow, 4 medium, 2 hairpin, 1 fast), 67% straight, avg radius 105 m, longest straight 400 m
- Wangan: 5.8 km, 5 corners (3 fast, 2 medium), 84% straight, avg radius 282 m, longest straight 1500 m
- Circuit: 2.9 km, 8 corners (3 slow, 2 medium, 1 hairpin, 2 fast), 64% straight, avg radius 129 m, longest straight 380 m
  Touge = tight downhill pass (short straights, hairpins) -> grip + agility.
  Mountain = longer pass with variety -> balance.
  Wangan = bayshore expressway (long straights, fast sweepers) -> power + low drag.
  Circuit = club track, mixed -> all-round.
