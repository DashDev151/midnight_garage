# Period Japanese used-car classified scans: harvest CLS

**Kept 22, rejected 30.** Every one of the 52 candidates was downloaded and viewed with
my own eyes before the decision. Nothing here is described from a caption. Rejections were
mostly one failure repeated: an undifferentiated grid of small photographs and price boxes
with no design on the page. The rest were tight crops with heavy moire, seller condition
photographs of a magazine's spine and fore-edge shot at an angle on cloth, and one blogger
post that turned out to be a Suzuki manufacturer brochure rather than a classified magazine.

Three magazines are represented:

| Publication | Issue | Files |
|---|---|---|
| Goo, Kyushu edition | August 1998 | 17 (`CLS_01` to `CLS_17`) |
| Weekly Car Sensor, Kanto edition | Vol.39, 11 October 2001 | 2 (`CLS_18`, `CLS_19`) |
| Weekly Car Sensor, Tokai edition | Vol.15, 17 April 2002 | 2 (`CLS_20`, `CLS_21`) |
| Car Sensor, national edition | Vol.273, 24 June 1996 | 1 (`CLS_22`) |

The Goo section is by far the deepest because it came from a blogger who photographed one
magazine thoroughly; the Car Sensor material came from back-issue auction listings, which
give excellent covers but only whatever interior pages a seller chose to photograph.

## The issue, and how it is dated

Every saved page belongs to one magazine: 中古車情報誌・グー【九州版】 (Goo, Kyushu
edition), published by Proto, covering Fukuoka, Saga and Yamaguchi.

Dating evidence, all read off the scans themselves rather than off any caption:

1. **The cover** (`CLS_01`) carries the masthead line 平成10年8月1日発行 月刊グー九州版
   (通巻25号): published 1 August 1998, monthly, cumulative issue 25. The cover badge
   reads '98.8.
2. The cover also prints 毎月24日発売 with a note that the on-sale date varies by region,
   and 次号は7月25日(土)発売.
3. Two separate inside pages (`CLS_16`, `CLS_17`) repeat that footer independently:
   来月は25日発売 / 次号は7月25日(土曜日)、書店・コンビニエンスストアでお求め下さい。
4. **Cross-check against a real calendar:** 25 July 1998 was indeed a Saturday. The
   nearest other years fail: 25 July 1997 was a Friday and 25 July 1999 a Sunday. The
   printed weekday and the printed year agree, so the year is not in doubt.
5. Corroborating internal marks: a Wagon R listed as 10年 (Heisei 10 = 1998) with 検13.1
   (shaken to January 2001), which is exactly the three years a new registration gets;
   an editorial spread on the R34 Skyline and the Toyota Progres, both launched May 1998.

So the issue went on sale in Kyushu around 25 June 1998 and is cover-dated August 1998.
Filenames use `1998-08`, the cover date.

All three source blog posts carry the same edition code prefix `38-` in the page gutters
(38-GA0042, 38-GA0280, 38-GB1491, 38-QZA101 and so on) and consecutive page numbers in
the 500s, which is what confirms the three posts are one physical magazine rather than
three.

# Notes on reading these pages

- Years in the small print are Heisei unless the car is an import, in which case the
  advert switches to the western year (the Saturns at 97年, the Lincoln at 79年, the
  Mercedes at 89年). Heisei 1 is 1989, so H5 is 1993 and H10 is 1998. Occasionally a
  pre-Heisei car is given in Showa (63年 is 1988, 48年 is 1973).
- 検 followed by two numbers is the shaken expiry in Heisei year and month, so 検11.4 is
  April 1999. 検2年付 means two years of fresh shaken are included in the price.
- Mileage is printed in 万K, so 7.8万K is 78,000 km. Some adverts print raw km instead.
- 万円 is man-yen, ten thousand yen, so 128万円 is 1,280,000 yen.
- ASK in the price box means the price is not printed and you telephone.
- 特選車 is the dealer's own featured or pick-of-the-lot label and gets a wider box.
- None of the dealers on these pages is a 絶版車 specialist. They are ordinary Kyushu
  lots, custom and lowrider shops, and one large chain, so the prices read as mainstream
  regional retail rather than as graded collector stock. That makes them a better baseline
  for the game's ordinary market than a specialist's asking prices would be.

# What was rejected

Thirty candidates were viewed and dropped:

- Nineteen were undifferentiated stock grids from the 1998 Goo issue, colour and
  monochrome, legible but with no design on the page beyond the section tab. Several
  carried prices; three such pages were kept as data and that is enough of them.
- Four were seller condition photographs of the Car Sensor Kanto and Tokai issues:
  the spine, the fore-edge and a cover corner, shot at a steep angle on black cloth with
  most of the frame given over to the cloth. They are not page scans. They were, however,
  useful, and their dating marks are cited above: they are where 週刊 Weekly Car Sensor,
  毎週木曜日発売, 読者応援定価 ¥250, RECRUIT and the 34,531台 listing count were read.
- Three were further Car Sensor Tokai interior spreads that duplicated what `CLS_21`
  already shows, or were plain dealer grids at an angle with the type too small to read.
- Two were tight crops rather than pages, with heavy moire from photographing halftone
  newsprint close up.
- Two were pages from a Suzuki Wagon R manufacturer brochure, posted by the same blogger
  and initially promising, but a brochure is not a classified magazine and is off this
  beat.

Not viewed and therefore not saved: five further images from the two auction galleries and
eight further Car Sensor cover photographs from other auction listings were downloaded but
collection was stopped before they were opened. Nothing unviewed has been saved, and
nothing unviewed is described anywhere in this manifest.

# What worked, for whoever picks this up next

Two routes produced everything here, and one route produced nothing.

**Route that produced the depth:** find one person who photographed a magazine, then read
their entire blog. minkara user 232669 photographed the August 1998 Goo Kyushu across
three consecutive posts and 17 of the 22 files here came from him. The way to walk a
minkara blog is not the index page, which is JavaScript-rendered, but the previous-post
and next-post links inside each post's HTML, which are plain anchors.

**Route that produced the breadth:** Yahoo Auctions completed-listing search for back
issues, in the used-car-magazine category 2084008100. Sellers photograph the cover and
often several interior spreads. The search-result thumbnail URL is the seller's original
photo with a query string appended; strip everything from `?pri=l` onward and the full
resolution original returns. Item-detail pages rate-limit hard after about a dozen
fetches; the search pages and the image CDN do not.

**Route that produced nothing:** keyword search for period content. minkara's blog search
is recency-weighted and returns 2026 posts for period keywords, and general web search
across ameblo, fc2, livedoor and hatena surfaced only the modern carsensor.net site. Do
not spend time there. Note also that searching カーセンサー together with バックナンバー
floods with false positives for a J-pop band's clothing line.

**Still worth having:** an interior page from a mid-1990s Car Sensor. Every 1995 and 1996
item found was a seller's cover photograph; nobody had photographed the inside of one.
