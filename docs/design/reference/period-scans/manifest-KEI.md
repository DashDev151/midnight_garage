# Period scans: kei cars and ordinary everyday metal (tag KEI)

**Kept for art: 6. Kept for prices (data): 12. Viewed and rejected or deleted: 55.**

Two publications, both regional and both from the cheap end of the market. Every file listed
below was downloaded, opened and read by me before it was kept. Prices are transcribed from the
saved pages, plus from pages I read but did not keep (recorded at the end so the figures are not
lost with the file).

## Conventions used throughout

- Prices are printed in 万円. 1万円 = 10,000 yen. A "19万円" car is 190,000 yen.
- Registration year (年式) is printed in Japanese era years. Heisei 1 = 1989, so "5年" = 1993,
  "10年" = 1998. Two-digit years of 48 to 63 are Showa: Showa 48 = 1973, Showa 63 = 1988.
  Imports are sometimes listed by western year instead ("97年", "86年"); noted where it happens.
- 検 is the shaken (roadworthiness) expiry, printed as Heisei year.month. "検13.1" means the
  certificate runs to January 2001.
- Mileage is printed as 万K (tens of thousands of km) or as a raw figure.
- 諸経費 / 諸費用 are the on-the-road fees charged on top of the advertised price.

# Publication 1: 月刊Goo 九州版, August 1998 issue

Regional edition of the Goo used-car classified magazine, published by Proto Corporation.
Covers Fukuoka, Saga and Yamaguchi. Cover price 250 yen. 7,967 cars listed in the issue.

**Dating evidence.** Four independent marks inside the scans agree.
1. The cover carries "'98.8" beside the masthead and the strapline 中古車情報誌・グー【九州版】.
2. The cover footer reads 毎月24日発売 and 次号は7月25日(土)発売. 25 July 1998 was indeed a
   Saturday, so the "next issue on the 25th, a Saturday" note is consistent with 1998 and not
   with the neighbouring years.
3. The masthead small print reads 平成10年8月1日発行 月刊グー九州版（通巻25号）. Heisei 10 = 1998.
4. The back cover advertises a Nissan U-Cars event on 7/11 (土) and 7/12 (日). 11 July 1998 was
   a Saturday.

So: cover-dated August 1998, on sale from about 24 to 25 June 1998. No mark disagrees.

# Publication 2: P!CARS (ピッカーズ), April 1992, Kyushu and Yamaguchi

A regional speciality-car information magazine covering Kyushu and Yamaguchi, cover price 380 yen.
Its value here is the 読者参加（個人売買）corner, a reader-to-reader private-sale classified
section, which is where the genuinely old and cheap cars sat.

**This issue is outside the 1995 to 2005 window and I am flagging that plainly.** I kept it
because the coordinator asked for prices on cars that had left the retail market by 2005, and a
1992 private-sale section is one of the few places those cars appear with a figure against them.
Treat these as 1992 prices, not as in-period prices.

**Dating evidence.** The cover prints "4" large with "1992 APRIL" beneath it and the price 380 yen.
Inside, an event listing gives 第3回北九州クラシックカーフェスティバル 4月11日(土) 12日(日);
11 April 1992 was a Saturday. A second listing gives 第1回クラシックカー・イン志高 4月26日(日);
26 April 1992 was a Sunday. Both weekday checks agree with 1992. The next-issue trailer is headed
次号（5月号）予告.

- **Daihatsu Mira TR-XX (L70), 550 cc.** No price found. The nearest is the 660 cc L200 TR-XX at

The reason is structural. Both publications are Kyushu titles that skew to speciality and
enthusiast stock; the 1998 Goo pages the blogger chose to photograph are dealer display adverts
rather than the cheap back sections, and the 1992 P!CARS private-sale corner is dominated by
imports and sports cars. A regional free classified paper, of the kind that carried plain
forecourt stock, is the right source for those three and I did not reach one.

# Leads found but not followed, for whoever picks this up

Two in-period regional titles surfaced on Yahoo Auctions that nobody appears to have scanned yet.
Both are exactly the right kind of publication for the cheap end.

- **月刊カーウォンテッド (CAR WANTED)**, published by ホームズカンパニー. A local used-car
  information magazine for **Ehime prefecture**. Back issues currently listed include Vol.117
  (June 1995), 118 (July 1995), 119 (August 1995), 121 (October 1995), 123 (December 1995),
  124 (January 1996), 126 (March 1996), 128 (May 1996), 141 (June 1997) and 142 (July 1997).
  A continuous run right through the early part of our window, from a prefecture nobody researches.
- **カーチョ**, a **Nara prefecture** used-car information magazine. Vol.91 and Vol.107 listed.

**Access note.** The Yahoo Auctions search endpoint
`https://auctions.yahoo.co.jp/search/search?p=<urlencoded>` responds normally to curl and the
listing titles parse out of the HTML. The individual item pages
(`page.auctions.yahoo.co.jp/jp/auction/<id>`, which redirect to `auctions.yahoo.co.jp/jp/auction/<id>`)
returned **HTTP 500 to both curl and WebFetch** from this machine, so I could not reach the
sellers' photographs to see whether they show inner pages or only covers. Anyone retrying should
expect to need a different route to the item pages.

**Structural warning that shaped this collection.** minkara caps uploaded images at a limited
resolution, so a page of forty small adverts photographed and posted there can be physically
illegible however good the original photograph was. The files kept here came through at 1280x960
and every price above was read at that size; I rejected nothing on resolution grounds, but I did
prefer pages with fewer, larger adverts when choosing what to keep, for exactly this reason.
