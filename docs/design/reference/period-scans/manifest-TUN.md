# Manifest: tuning magazines and parts catalogues (TUN)

**Kept 5, rejected 9.** Three of the rejections were viewed and turned down on
quality or subject; six were turned down on measured pixel size or on falling
outside the 1990 to 2005 band. Every kept file below was opened and read by eye
before it was kept.

All five kept files are 900x1200 photographs of open catalogue pages, in colour,
sharp, evenly lit and square enough to read. Four are from one object, the HKS
`SUPER TECHNOLOGY HIGH-LIGHTS 1994` catalogue, and they show four different
subjects rather than four views of the same page.

Prices read off these pages, plus one price list read off an archived 1999 HKS
web page that produced no image worth keeping, are collected in
[Prices transcribed](#prices-transcribed) at the end.

**For parts pricing, `parts-prices.md` in this folder supersedes this manifest.**
It carries the full sweep, about 130 transcribed period prices across exhaust,
suspension, brakes, intake and turbo, engine management, drivetrain, wheels and
interior, from HKS, Endless, Cusco and RAYS between 1994 and 2004, sorted into
the ladder the game needs. The section below is kept only because it records
what is legible on these specific image files.

## A note on what is not here

The beat asked for magazine scans as well as catalogues: `Option`, `Option2`,
`CARBOY`, `REV SPEED`, `ドリフト天国`, and the small tuning-shop adverts at the
back of them. None were saved. Yahoo Auctions listings for period issues of all
of those were located by title and issue (`Option` 1992-10 and 1995-12, `CARBOY`
1990-09, `ドリフト天国` 2003-11 and 2004-11, `OPTION2` 2001), but Yahoo began
returning HTTP 500 on every auction item page part way through the session and
did not recover, so their photographs could never be reached. The listing search
endpoint kept working throughout, which is how the issues are known to exist.
That route is the one to resume from, not a reason to think the material is
scarce.

## 1994-00_hks-super-technology-highlights_cover-jza80-supra-TUN_01.jpg

- **Source page:** Yahoo Auctions listing `k1222103176`,
  `https://page.auctions.yahoo.co.jp/jp/auction/k1222103176`
- **Direct image:** `https://auctions.c.yimg.jp/images.auctions.yahoo.co.jp/image/dr000/auc0203/user/953e7a38373e61b218b4883ef3a339453bc21fd6f71fa2a63de0d939d6f3890c/i-img900x1200-17728746624668obx7sd61073.jpg`
- **Dating:** the cover itself prints `1994` in large figures directly beneath
  the strapline `Pursuing the Ultimate in Engine Performance and Efficiency.
  H.K.S Product Company Limited.`, with `SPECIAL EDITION` set top right. The
  interior pages of the same object carry a second, independent mark agreeing
  with it: a repeated masthead device reading `PARTS & SYSTEM '94 Powered by
  HKS` over `1994 Segments of the Power`. Year certain, month not printed
  anywhere I could see, hence `1994-00`.
- **On the page:** front cover. The HKS blocked logotype in solid black, then
  `SUPER TECHNOLOGY` and `HIGH-LIGHTS` in a heavy condensed grotesque, on a
  white ground. Below, a single hero photograph of a black JZA80 Supra, lit from
  above on a plain sweep with a hard shadow pooled under the car. The paper
  carries light handling wear and a crease at the top right corner, which reads
  as age rather than as damage to the image.
- **Prices:** none on this page.

## 1994-00_hks-super-technology-highlights_intercooler-engine-parts-TUN_02.jpg

- **Source page:** Yahoo Auctions listing `k1222103176` (as above)
- **Direct image:** `https://auctions.c.yimg.jp/images.auctions.yahoo.co.jp/image/dr000/auc0203/user/953e7a38373e61b218b4883ef3a339453bc21fd6f71fa2a63de0d939d6f3890c/i-img900x1200-17728746624917y654qa61073.jpg`
- **Dating:** same object as TUN_01, and this spread carries the `PARTS & SYSTEM
  '94 Powered by HKS` / `1994 Segments of the Power` device in full, set as a
  diagonal script lock-up across the gutter. Model references on the spread are
  consistent with it: FD3S RX-7, BNR32, 1JZ-GTE, 2JZ, 3S-GT, 4A-GE, RB26DETT.
- **On the page:** a two-page spread, black ground throughout. Left page runs
  large cutaway-style photography of a turbocharger compressor wheel and a blow
  off valve, with a `T300S` specification block. Right page is headed
  `INTERCOOLER SERIES` in outlined display capitals, and carries colour product
  photography of an intercooler kit, intercooler piping and a metal cylinder
  head gasket, plus three rows of piston kits captioned `RB26DETT`, `1JZ-GTE`
  and `4A-GE`. Between them sits the best drawn thing on the spread: a set of
  monochrome technical cross-section diagrams of head gasket sealing types
  (`BEAD TYPE`, `GROMMET TYPE`, `STOPER TYPE`), each a clean line drawing of the
  bead and stopper profiles. `ENGINE PARTS` runs vertically down the gutter in
  Japanese-style vertical display type.
- **Prices:** one, see the table below.

## 1992-00_hks-zero-r-catalogue_cover-TUN_05.jpg

- **Source page:** Yahoo Auctions listing `p1236129025`,
  `https://page.auctions.yahoo.co.jp/jp/auction/p1236129025`
- **Direct image:** `https://auctions.c.yimg.jp/images.auctions.yahoo.co.jp/image/dr000/auc0207/user/953e7a38373e61b218b4883ef3a339453bc21fd6f71fa2a63de0d939d6f3890c/i-img900x1200-17833271589999famyjh26.jpg`
- **Dating, and a caveat:** **no printed date appears anywhere on the pages I
  saw**, so the year in the filename is inferred and should be treated as such.
  What the object does carry: the identical strapline to the dated 1994
  catalogue above, `Pursuing the Ultimate in Engine Performance and Efficiency.
  H.K.S Product Company Limited.`, which places it in the same house style and
  the same broad period; and a subject, the HKS ZERO-R, which was HKS's own
  R32-based complete car, built in tiny numbers in the early 1990s. The
  specification page of the same catalogue (downloaded, viewed, then rejected on
  quality) listed an F-CON plus VPC plus GCC II engine management stack and 17
  inch wheels on 255/40-17, all consistent with that. **Honest range: 1991 to
  1994**, most probably 1992. Filename uses `1992-00` because the convention has
  no way to express a range. If a printed date later turns up, correct it.
- **On the page:** front cover. A custom `ZERO R` logotype, the `ZERO` in an
  outlined squared-off face and the `R` drawn as a swept ribbon, reversed out
  white over a photograph of a silver R32-shaped coupe on a tree-lined road,
  shot low and close from the front three-quarter. The car wears a period white
  Japanese registration plate. Print grain is visible, which is the object and
  not the photograph of it.
- **Prices:** none on this page.

basis is stated from the column headings and from period practice, and the
uncertainty is flagged rather than hidden.

product photograph, in the Japanese convention of 本体価格, that is the unit

not stated on the pages in frame; period Japanese catalogue practice of 1994

## Rejected, and why

Recorded so the same ground is not covered twice.

- **HKS ZERO-R catalogue, specification spread** (`p1236129025`, image 2).
  Downloaded and viewed. A hero photograph of the car bleeding across the gutter
  with full specification and equipment tables beside it, genuinely useful for
  parts vocabulary since it lists every HKS part fitted to the complete car. Cut
  on quality: the left page is lost to the gutter, there is visible page curl,
  and it carries no prices, so the price-list exemption does not apply.
- **HKS ZERO-R catalogue, back cover** (`p1236129025`, image 3). Viewed. Branch
  addresses and telephone numbers over a motorway photograph, type far too small
  to read. No design interest.
- **Three images from a minkara post, `古本まゆ`,
  `https://minkara.carview.co.jp/userid/1865435/blog/49202590/`.** One viewed: a
  Nissan Cherry Coupe manufacturer advertisement of about 1971. Wrong decade and
  wrong beat, being a factory advert rather than tuning print. The other two
  from the same post were not opened once the post's subject was established.
- **Four images from Internet Archive captures of `hks-power.co.jp`, 1999 to
  2001** (`communique/9812/image/1-1.jpg`, `communique/9901/image/3s_ch_gasket.JPG`,
  `communique/9901/image/cam_1jz.JPG`,
  `goods_master/denshi_parts/camp8_28/img/kinou/ANALOG.jpg`). Downloaded and
  measured rather than viewed: 371x244, 500x131, 331x263 and 150x108 pixels.
  Period website images of the era are simply too small to be reference art.
  **The archived maker sites remain worth mining for prices, as the extension
  kit table above shows, but not for pictures.**
