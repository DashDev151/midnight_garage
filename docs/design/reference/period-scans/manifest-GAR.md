# Period scans: workshops, garages, forecourts and car culture (tag GAR)

**Kept 4, rejected 78.** All four are colour pages, viewed at full size before saving. Rejections
were mostly out of period (a large pool of 1970s-80s CARBOY and a 2010 CARBOY issue that read as
period at thumbnail size), plus monochrome technical articles with no design on them, undersized
images, and seller boilerplate slides.

Every image here is a photograph of a printed magazine page, taken by a Yahoo Auctions seller
listing the issue as a back number. The page is therefore slightly curved at the gutter and lit by
room light; none is a flatbed scan. Colour rendition is close but not colour-managed, so treat the
hues below as reported rather than measured.

Three of the four come from one issue, dated from its own imprint block. That issue is
オプション2 7月臨時増刊「ドリフト天国」, published 平成9年7月25日 (25 July 1997) by
株式会社ティーズ・クラブ, 定価500円. The fourth is an earlier Option2 and is dated separately below.

- 1200 x 1200 px.

**Dating.** Pages 6 and 7 of the issue above. The running foot on the left page reads "7 OPTION2".
The contents page of the same issue (item 2 below, same seller, same lot) carries the imprint
平成9年7月25日, so this page is 25 July 1997. Independently consistent: the cars are S14 Silvia
(pre-facelift), A31 Cefiro, C33 Laurel and RPS13 180SX, all current or recent in 1997.

**What is in frame.** Colour throughout. A two-page feature on full resprays (オールペン), run under
a hand-lettered script logo "Painting Body Tune!" in black on a pale blue panel.

Left page: a cut-out side view of an orange-red 180SX on white, labelled 蛍光色 / COLOR NAME
スカーレット; below it a cut-out of a bright grass-green S14 Silvia labelled ソリッド with the colour
name 公衆電話グリーン (public telephone green), which is exactly the colour of a 1990s NTT payphone.
Each car carries a black spec box in white type listing the shop that sprayed it, the process, the
merits and the owner (塩田正輝 25歳, 前田剛 24歳), with the shop's phone number set in the block. A
small red hand-drawn arrow and the line "NTTはエライ!" points at the green car. Owner portraits are
postage-stamp size, men in their twenties squatting beside their cars on tarmac.

Right page: a large photograph of a deep wine-red S14 Silvia bonnet with pearl flake catching the
light, shot low, plus two wine-red C33 Laurels and an A31 Cefiro parked in a line on wet grey
tarmac under overcast light. The headline オールペンリーダー大集合 is set in heavy yellow outlined
display type running diagonally across the page over a red starburst carrying "NEW" in white. A
second hand-lettered headline ソウルペイント sits in magenta with a white keyline. A COLOR NAME box
bottom-right gives ブラウンディワイン with three owners listed (花島一弥 24歳, 足立和貴 23歳,
中村賢二 23歳). Bottom-right photograph: three young men in black T-shirts, one with a skull print,
sitting and leaning on their cars.

Palette of the page itself: white ground, pale blue and pale pink tint panels, black rules, yellow
and magenta display type, one red starburst. Prices are printed in the spec boxes (resprays quoted
in the ¥300,000 to ¥500,000 band, with a ¥300,000 paint-blemish correction line).

**Why it is here.** This is the closest thing found to a paint-shop menu: named colours, the shop
that applied them, the process, and the price, all on one designed spread.

## 1997-07_option2-drift-tengoku_contents-masthead-GAR_02.jpg

**Dating.** Certain. The imprint block at the foot of the page reads
オプション2 7月臨時増刊 ドリフト天国 / 発行 平成9年7月25日 / 定価500円(本体476円 送料116円) /
発行人 細田大二郎 / 編集人 斉藤政夫 / 発行元 株式会社ティーズ・クラブ, 〒151 東京都渋谷区千駄ヶ谷
3-26-5 / 発売元 株式会社三栄書房, 〒169 東京都新宿区北新宿4-8-16. Both postcodes are the old
three-digit form, which Japan replaced in February 1998, consistent with a 1997 print date.

**What is in frame.** Colour. The contents page, and the single most designed page of the four.

Across the top, a strip of three colour photographs: a night car meet with five or six cars nose-in
under orange sodium light, people standing between them in dark clothing, the tarmac wet enough to
throw reflections; a man in a red jacket leaning into an open car door in daylight; a white AE86
Levin parked on a circuit apron against a blue steel building.

The contents list runs in two columns, black body type with the article titles set in blue, red,
yellow and green, so each entry reads as two colours at once. Above it sits the same hand-lettered
"Painting Body Tune!" script as item 1.

Bottom left is the piece a letterer would want: the ドリフト天国 logo, set as fat rounded kanji in
red, blue, green and yellow over a radiating red-and-white sunburst on a pale green panel, with
"D-SHOCK" beneath in heavy red and black sans, and a curved line of type arcing over the top
(スミからスミまでドリフトネタのオンパレード!). To its right, the imprint block in small mono type,
including the staff list with photographers, writers, illustrators and a design studio credited.

Right edge: a red BRIDE bucket seat shot against black for a full-page advert, with the ZETA price
list in white and the BRIDE wordmark on a black panel.

Page palette: white ground, pale green panel, red and blue rules, a red bucket seat, and the
four-colour logo. The paper has yellowed slightly at the edge.

## 1997-07_option2-drift-tengoku_re-amemiya-shop-GAR_03.jpg

- Source page: https://auctions.yahoo.co.jp/jp/auction/d1237052555
- Direct image: https://auctions.c.yimg.jp/images.auctions.yahoo.co.jp/image/dr000/auc0207/user/f926baec5f7c2f16911b12667f28e6639e0aa1a1ebf48dd0336be0b5123e2b01/i-img1200x1200-17840083548687it2wyf1292.jpg
- 1200 x 1200 px.

**Dating.** Pages 18 and 19 of the 25 July 1997 issue; the running foot on the left page reads
"19 OPTION2". The contents list on item 2 confirms the article at page 18
(ビッグニュース! RE雨宮がサポートするドリ野郎).

**What is in frame.** Colour. A shop feature, and the only workshop interior in this set.

The car is an FC3S RX-7 in a two-tone blue and magenta scheme, shot in side view on a grey concrete
apron with green trees behind, and again head-on. Door lettering reads 雨宮 in a red panel; the
bonnet carries "REsuper G" in white script.

The person photographs are the useful part. On the right page, a man (鶴田隆之 25才, 群馬県在住)
stands beside the car in a one-piece pale blue-grey work coverall, zipped to the chest, sleeves
down, hands at his sides. His name is set in a black box with a yellow keyline. Bottom left of the
left page, a second man (雨宮勇美 of RE雨宮) is photographed head and shoulders in a blue nylon team
blouson with magenta sleeve panels and embroidered sponsor patches at the chest, on a pale blue
tint panel with a rounded black keyline.

Bottom right of the right page is the workshop shot, small in frame but legible: the inside of a
corrugated-roof shed with exposed roof trusses and unpainted walls, daylight coming in from the
open end, four or five cars parked close together on a bare concrete floor including a blue and red
liveried racing RX-7 with a number roundel, wheels and equipment stacked along the wall. The
caption names the bodyshop as 松本板金 and says it is so sympathetic to 走り屋 that it seems to exist
entirely for them.

Left page also carries a monochrome-ish engine bay, an intercooler, and a boxed ENGINE&DRIVE-TRAIN
/ SUSPENSION&BRAKE / TIRE&WHEEL / INTERIOR&EXTERIOR spec list in small black type.

Page palette: white ground, pale blue tint panels, black keylines, a red-orange banner carrying
カラーでドーリドリ!! in white reversed type, orange and magenta headline type.

## 1991-00_option2_original-colour-wangan-pa-GAR_04.jpg

- Source page: https://auctions.yahoo.co.jp/jp/auction/d1191801685
- Direct image: https://auctions.c.yimg.jp/images.auctions.yahoo.co.jp/image/dr000/auc0107/user/f926baec5f7c2f16911b12667f28e6639e0aa1a1ebf48dd0336be0b5123e2b01/i-img1200x1200-17519428452925it67dl59931.jpg
- 1200 x 1200 px.

**Dating: 1991, month not established, and looser than the three above.** Marks used, in order of
weight:

1. The reader-submission block prints the address 〒169 東京都新宿区北新宿4-8-16 三栄書房
   オプション2編集部, and offers an "OPT2 sticker" to contributors. The three-digit postcode places
   it before February 1998. The running foot reads "151 OPTION".
2. The body text says a car will appear at the スーパーバトル in 間瀬 on 9月1日. Circuit meetings of
   this kind ran on Sundays. Cross-checking a real calendar, 1 September fell on a Sunday in 1991
   but on a Saturday in 1990, a Friday in 1989 and a Tuesday in 1992. That points at 1991 and, since
   the event is written about as forthcoming, at an issue on sale in August or September 1991.
3. Newest cars visible: S13 Silvia Q's, RPS13 180SX (from 1989), FC3S RX-7, AE86 Levin. There is no
   FD3S RX-7 anywhere on the spread, and the FD went on sale in October 1991. That is consistent
   with, and independently supports, a mid-1991 date.

I have not found a cover date or issue number, so treat this as 1991 with a residual range of
roughly 1990 to 1992.

**What is in frame.** Colour, and the loudest page design of the four. A two-page feature on
owner-chosen respray colours, staged in three different places, which is why it is worth keeping
alongside item 1.

Left page, 間瀬サーキット: a red ADVAN-liveried FC3S RX-7 and a deep green AE86 Levin on circuit
tarmac with grass verges and stacked black tyre walls behind; below, a line of four cars parked on
a grey paddock apron in pale yellow, red and blue. A lavender-painted S13 Silvia with a race number
on the door sits on the pit apron. Headline 間瀬サーキット is set in enormous magenta and blue
outlined display kanji running vertically, over green and blue colour blocks.

Right page top, 峠: four cars photographed on wet tarmac at a mountain pass, an ice-blue S13 Silvia
Q's, a pale cream AE86 Levin, a mid-blue AE86 and a red AE86, all carrying "M factory" side
decals, each with a small circular portrait of its owner and a printed respray cost, ¥180,000,
¥150,000 and ¥230,000. A speech bubble reads TEAM M Factoryです。ヨロシクッ!

Right page bottom, 湾岸: the night material. A lemon-yellow RPS13 180SX on BBS-style mesh wheels
parked side-on at a parking area at night, artificial light raking along the flank, a low building
with shuttered windows behind and two young men standing in the shadow at the left edge watching.
Beside it, a blue metallic S13 Silvia with a rear wing, photographed from the front three-quarter
at night on block paving, mercury-vapour light picking out the metallic in the paint. The running
text calls it 某PA and says that at weekends the place is packed with young 最高速 obsessives. A
black bar carries 湾岸はこのくらいの色じゃないと目立てない in white reversed type; the word 湾岸 is
set again in huge pale lavender kanji; a magenta hand-lettered headline reads
鮮やかなペインティングが照明に輝くとき!

Page palette: white ground with saturated red, green, blue and yellow blocks at the page edges, a
yellow band across the foot, black bars with reversed white type, magenta and lavender display
lettering, white speech bubbles with black type. Busy, high-contrast and almost entirely
hand-composed.
