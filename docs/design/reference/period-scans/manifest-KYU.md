# manifest-KYU: classic and discontinued-car media (旧車 / 絶版車)

**Kept 11. Viewed and rejected 9.** Every file below was downloaded, opened and
looked at before it was kept; the nine rejects were opened, judged and deleted.
Ten of the eleven are kept as art (designed pages); one is kept as data
(`KYU_11`, a price grid).

Date range covered: October 1991 to April 2000, plus one 1993 Car Sensor page.
Publications covered: Nostalgic Hero (Geibunsha), Old-timer (Yaesu Shuppan),
Car Sensor (Recruit), Tipo / 絶版国産スポーツカー (Neko Publishing), 別冊宝島
(Takarajimasha), 名車グラフィティ (Tokuma Shoten), OLD WHEELS / CARTOP MOOK
(Kotsu Times), 絶版車購入ガイド (Naigai Shuppan), CROSS ROAD (Okinawa).

## Provenance note

Ten of the eleven come from one minkara blogger, 赤い荒鷲 (user 3658097), who
keeps a 310-post 資料 ("source material") category of photographs of his own
magazine collection, catalogued by year and title. He is an Isuzu Gemini owner,
so the right-hand page of a spread is often the Isuzu content that made him
photograph it; the left-hand page is normally the cover or the feature opener,
and that is usually where the design is. This is stated plainly so nobody later
mistakes the Isuzu bias for a fact about the magazines.

minkara caps blog-image uploads at 800 px on the long edge. That is the ceiling
on every file here, not a property of the original photographs. Body copy is
therefore not readable; mastheads, display type, cover lines and captions are.

## KYU_07

- **File:** `2000-04_old-timer-51_eagle-sayama-specialist-advert-KYU_07.jpg`
- **Source page:** https://minkara.carview.co.jp/userid/3658097/blog/48610536/
- **Image:** https://cdn.snsimg.carview.co.jp/minkara/blog/000/048/610/536/48610536/p1.jpg
- **Dating:** in-scan and good. The left page is the magazine's own index
  supplement and prints `1991-2000` down the spine edge plus
  `創刊50号突破記念特別付録 INDEX（創刊号-No.50総索引）`, which places the parent
  issue at No.51 and the year at 2000. The poster adds 平成12年4月1日発行, 八重洲
  出版, 1,000 yen with the supplement. Recorded as 2000-04 with confidence.
- **On the page:** the only genuine 旧車専門店 dealer advert I found, and the
  reason this file is kept. Left, the INDEX supplement cover: a red ground with
  `1991-2000` set enormous and vertical, an `Old-timer` masthead, `INDEX` in
  heavy display, and a tiled grid of thirty-odd miniature Old-timer covers.
  Right, a full-page advert for EAGLE SAYAMA (Saitama), headed
  「新・旧イスズ車常時100台在庫」 (new and old Isuzu, 100 cars always in stock) over
  a dark green ground carrying about thirty-five colour photographs of stock:
  117 Coupé, Bellett, Bellett GT, Florian and Gemini, each with a caption strip.
  A magenta strapline offers 「オリジナル＆純正パーツ、鈑金・整備・レストア・チューニング
  など」. The foot of the page has two photographs of the workshop interior, the
  EAGLE SAYAMA logo with a cartoon eagle mascot, tel 042-955-4711 / fax
  042-955-4721, a separate parts line 042-955-5781, and a hand-drawn location map.
  The car grid on its own is the phonebook pattern; the masthead, the strapline,
  the mascot and the drawn map are what earn it.
- **Prices:** each stock photograph has a price printed in its caption strip. At
  800 px they render as illegible smudges of about the right shape. I zoomed and
  could not read a single one reliably, so none are transcribed. **If this page
  can be re-sourced at higher resolution it is the single most valuable page in
  this beat**, because it is a specialist classic lot quoting real asking prices,
  and for a genuine 旧車 the specialist price is often the only price there was.

## KYU_10

- **File:** `1993-00_carsensor-vol220_gemini-ucar-feature-KYU_10.jpg`
- **Source page:** https://minkara.carview.co.jp/userid/3658097/blog/49121120/
- **Image:** https://cdn.snsimg.carview.co.jp/minkara/blog/000/049/121/120/49121120/p1.jpg
- **Dating: I disagree with the poster and the disagreement matters.** He titles
  it 「1992年」 and writes 平成4年頃 (about 1992). The page footer, which I have
  read at magnification, prints `60—CAR SENSOR Vol.220`. The body copy states
  that Isuzu has now stopped building passenger cars and that the current Gemini
  is an OEM-supplied car based on the Honda Domani; the Domani-based Gemini went
  on sale in 1993, so the issue cannot be 1992. Interpolating from the known
  anchor Car Sensor Vol.300 = July 1997 at the roughly twenty-issues-a-year
  cadence implied by the poster's own Vol.162 ≈ 1990 gives Vol.220 ≈ mid-1993.
  **Recorded as 1993, month unknown**, with a stated range of 1993 to early 1994.
- **On the page:** a single portrait page and a genuine period Car Sensor, which
  is the seam this whole library is about. Top two thirds is a full-bleed night
  photograph of a Gemini on a wet European-looking shopping street with
  pedestrians and lit `Renner BIER` and `Il Salotto` signs. Over it sits the
  section furniture: a `U-CAR 人気 年式別徹底研究` masthead in a black box,
  `SCENE 210 ISUZU ジェミニ` in a reversed tab, and a `Report／松下 宏` byline.
  Below, a heavy display headline 「Uカー市場で注目！最後の"いすゞスポーツ"」, a
  boxed 【キャラクター紹介】 subhead, three columns of body text, and a small
  cut-out of a red ZZ/R with a caption.
- **Prices:** none on this page. It is the editorial half of the year-by-year
  used-car study; the price tables sit elsewhere in the issue.

  offered (「アフターバッチリ、3ヶ月保障付」). Not a classic specialist. The one
  classic-relevant car on the page is priced as ordinary old stock, which is
