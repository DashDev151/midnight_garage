# Period scans: reference library

Photographed scans of Japanese car print media from roughly 1990 to 2005:
used-car classified magazines (Car Sensor, Goo), classic-car magazines
(Nostalgic Hero, Old Timer), tuning magazines (Option, CARBOY, REV SPEED,
Drift Tengoku), aftermarket parts catalogues, and period photographs of
workshops, forecourts and signage.

Collected as **reference** for the art direction and as a source of period
prices. Two uses, kept separate in the manifests, because they want opposite
things: a page can be beautiful and priceless, or ugly and full of figures.

## What is here

Images are named `YYYY-MM_publication_subject-TAG_NN.jpg`, where `YYYY-00`
means the year is known and the month is not. Every file was viewed before it
was kept, and dated from marks inside the scan (cover date, spine, the
`次号発売日` footer with its printed weekday cross-checked against a real
calendar, volume number, or a dated advert) rather than from the caption of
whoever posted it.

One manifest per collecting beat, each recording per file: source page, direct
image URL, the dating evidence, what is physically on the page, and any prices
legible on it.

| Manifest | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| `manifest-CLS.md` | Used-car classifieds, 1995 to 2002 |
| `manifest-KYU.md` | Classic and discontinued-car media |
| `manifest-TUN.md` | Tuning magazines and parts catalogues |
| `manifest-KEI.md` | Kei cars and ordinary metal |
| `manifest-GAR.md` | Workshops, forecourts, signage, culture |

## The bar

Not volume. A page earns its place by being **designed**: illustration, a
hand-drawn mascot, hand-lettering, display typography, a photo-collage header,
a cutaway or exploded diagram, a decorative border, a strong photograph. Colour
is a plus and not a requirement. An undifferentiated grid of small photographs
and figures is a phonebook, and is kept only when the prices on it are worth
having, labelled as data rather than art.
