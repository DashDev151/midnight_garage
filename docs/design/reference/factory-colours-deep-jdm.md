# Factory colours: the fourteen recognisable JDM cars

Research pass covering the roster entries where a player recognises a colour on sight. Method
inherited from `factory-colours-research.md`: gazoo.com per-grade catalogue pages are the reliable
source, colour-family headings are shade evidence, and 221616.com, goo-net index pages, ant-llc
naming and paintref.com are not to be trusted.

Two further source rules, one confirmed here and one carried in from a parallel pass:

- **gtrusablog.com and paintref.com both return HTTP 403 to a fetch.** Their content is still
  reachable through search-result snippets, which is how the R32 production shares below were got.
- **Cross-market name matching by paint code is unreliable.** A code confirms that a colour exists
  and what finish it has; it never licenses importing a name or a description from another market.
  Mitsubishi's R25 is Kutani Red in Japan and Morocco Red Pearl in the United States.
  `importarchive.com` is an American-market archive and is useful only for finish and shade
  evidence attached to an already-established JDM name.

**Scope: thirteen cars.** The MR2 SW20 (rosterNo 50) was researched to full depth by an earlier
pass and its section here is a cross-check only, not a second source of truth.

**Nothing here is authored into content.** Where a shade could not be established from a source it
says so rather than guessing.

Sections are in roster order. Each carries the roster's own year window at the top, because a
colour list is only true for a dated grade.

## 42. Toyota Sprinter Trueno GT-APEX (AE86), 1985-1987

**Gazoo's catalogue does not reach back this far.** Its Sprinter Trueno index starts at the AE92.
The best source found is NostalgicHero's AE86 model-by-model breakdown, which is code-level and
splits zenki from kouki. The roster window is entirely **kouki** (the May 1985 minor change onward),
which matters because every code changed at that point.

| name | code | shade | iconic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High-Tech Two-Tone | **2T7** (kouki) | white upper body over a black lower body and bonnet: the "panda". Composed of White 041 and Black 202 | **yes, the definitive AE86 colour** |
| White | 041 | plain solid white | yes |
| Black | 202 | plain solid black; a kouki addition, introduced on the Trueno as a special edition | no |
| Red | 3E6 | bright solid red | no |
| Silver Metallic | 148 | plain metallic silver | no |

**Do not use the zenki codes for a 1985-1987 car.** The pre-1985 equivalents are High-Tech Two-Tone
**2N2** (White 038 over Black 202), White 038, Silver Metallic 147 and Red 3D7. The names are
identical and the mixes are not, which is exactly the trap this window creates. A yellow existed
early and was gone by the kouki cars.

**The two-tone was standard-selectable on GT-APEX**, which is the roster's grade; on lesser grades
(GT-V, GT, and later SE and SR) it was a paid option.

**Confidence: partial.** The codes and the zenki/kouki split are solid and from a specialist
source. The list is likely complete but was not confirmed against a dated factory catalogue, and no
per-grade catalogue page exists for cross-checking.

**Gap:** whether any two-tone other than white-over-black was offered on the kouki Trueno GT-APEX
was not established. No source gave a full kouki catalogue list, only the codes that changed.

Sources: https://nosweb.jp/nostalgichero/articles/detail/3965 ,
https://nosweb.jp/nostalgichero/articles/detail/3963

## 52. Mazda RX-7 Type R (FD3S, '92), 1992-1995

The roster window crosses one list change, at **August 1993**, and the change is substantive: the
yellow leaves, the white arrives, and four colours are promoted from paid option to standard.

**Dec 1991 to Aug 1993, Efini RX-7 Type R:**

| name | Japanese | shade | status | iconic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vintage Red | ヴィンテージレッド | a deep solid red, darker and more restrained than a fire-engine red; the FD's launch and brochure colour | standard | **yes, the FD's own colour** |
| Montego Blue Mica | モンテゴブルーマイカ | **filed under GREEN, not blue**: a dark blue-green mica, teal-leaning rather than a true blue. This is the single most useful shade fact on this car | option | yes |
| Brilliant Black | ブリリアントブラック | solid black with a clear coat, no pearl or flake | option | no |
| Silver Stone Metallic | シルバーストーンメタリック | plain metallic silver | option | no |
| Competition Yellow Mica | コンペティションイエローマイカ | bright saturated yellow with a mica sparkle | option | **yes, and window-limited** |

**Aug 1993 to Mar 1995, Type R:** Competition Yellow Mica is **gone**. Chaste White (シャストホワイト,
a plain solid white) arrives, and Vintage Red, Montego Blue Mica, Brilliant Black and Silver Stone
Metallic are all now standard. Every colour additionally offered with the Hi-Reflex Coat finish
(ハイレフコート塗装) as a paid option line.

**The yellow only existed on the first twenty months of Type R.** For a roster car dated from 1992
that is fine, but a pool authored across 1992-1995 should not treat it as always-available.

**Confidence: solid** on names, dates and the option/standard split, from two dated grade
catalogues.

**Gap:** no paint codes were pulled, and none should be guessed. The Dec 1991 list returned five
colours with no white at all, which is either genuine (white came at the 1993 change) or a missed
row; **it was not confirmed either way**.

Sources: https://gazoo.com/catalog/maker/MAZDA/EFINI_RX7/199112/2501590/ ,

## 57. Subaru Impreza WRX STi Version (GC8), 1994-2000

The roster window spans six numbered STi Versions and the palette moves under it. Two dated grade
catalogues were pulled, one from each half.

**Sep 1997 to Sep 1998, WRX STi Version IV:**

| Active Red | アクティブレッド | bright solid red | no |

| Black Mica | ブラック・マイカ | black with a mica sparkle | no |

**Sep 1999 to Aug 2000, WRX STi Version VI:**

| name | Japanese | shade | iconic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pure White | ピュアホワイト | plain solid white | no |
| Arctic Silver Metallic | アークティックシルバー・メタリック | cool bright metallic silver | no |

| Cool Grey Metallic | クールグレー・メタリック | mid grey metallic with a cool cast | no |

**The blue name changes across the window, and none of them is "WR Blue".** Version IV has Royal

months does list a blue. That may be genuine (the grades really did differ) or it may be a missed
row. An Impreza with no blue option is surprising enough that it should not be authored on this
evidence alone.

**Gap:** Versions I, II, III and V were not checked; nor were any paint codes. The window is six
years long and only two points in it were sampled.

## 69. Mitsubishi Lancer Evo VIII MR (CT9A), 2004-2005

Two dated GSR Evolution VIII MR catalogues, Feb-Apr 2004 and Apr 2004 to Mar 2005, carry an
**identical four-colour list**. This is the narrowest palette of any car in this pass.

| name | Japanese | shade | iconic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| White Solid | ホワイトソリッド | flat solid white, no pearl; the works-rally white | **yes, the Evo's own colour** |
| Red Solid | レッドソリッド | flat solid red, no metallic or pearl | yes |
| Cool Silver Metallic | クールシルバーメタリック | bright metallic silver with a cool cast | no |

**Gap:** no paint codes. The RS grades were not checked and are likely narrower still (RS cars

## 77. Nissan Skyline GT-R (BNR32), 1992-1994

Two dated gazoo grade catalogues sit inside the roster window and they carry an identical list: the
V-spec of Feb 1993 to Aug 1993, and the V-spec II of Feb 1994 to Jan 1995. Five colours, all
manufacturer standard, all with the Super Fine Coating finish.

**The BNR32 list is not the same across the car's whole life, and the roster window sits on the
later half of it.** The Aug 1989 to Aug 1991 GT-R carried Red Pearl Metallic, **Dark Blue Pearl**
(TH1), Black Pearl Metallic, **Jet Silver Metallic** (KG1) and Gun Grey Metallic. By Feb 1993 the
blue and the Jet Silver are gone and Crystal White and Spark Silver have replaced them. Neither
Dark Blue Pearl nor Jet Silver belongs on a 1992-1994 car.

| name | Japanese | code | shade | iconic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gun Grey Metallic | ガングレーメタリック | KH2 | dark charcoal metallic with a cool blue cast; reads near-black in shade and clearly blue-grey in sun. Filed under GREY. **37 per cent of 1992 BNR32 production**, and the colour Nissan put in the brochures | **yes, the R32's signature** |
| Crystal White | クリスタルホワイト | 326 | plain solid white, no pearl | no |
| Red Pearl | レッドパール | AH3 | deep red with a fine pearl, darker and less orange than Nissan's Super Red | no |
| Black Pearl Metallic | ブラックパールメタリック | 732 | black with a fine metallic pearl flake, not a flat black | no |
| Spark Silver Metallic | スパークシルバーメタリック | KL0 | bright clean metallic silver | no |

**No Midnight Purple on a BNR32.** One aggregator (32gt-r.com) lists LP2 Midnight Purple, Active
Red and "Aztec Red" for the R32; it contradicts both gazoo's dated catalogues and the paint-code
guides on the codes it shares (it maps TH1 to Active Red where every other source has TH1 as Dark
Blue Pearl). **Treat that page as unreliable and do not mine it.** LP2 is an R33-era colour.

**Confidence: solid** on the five names, from two dated grade catalogues that agree. Codes are
cross-referenced from paint-code guides rather than the catalogue itself.

**Gap:** **1992 itself is not directly catalogued.** Gazoo's grades jump from Aug 1989-Aug 1991 to
Feb 1993, so the Aug 1991 minor-change car has no grade page. The five-colour Feb 1993 list is the
best available proxy for a 1992 car and the code evidence supports it (Crystal White 326 and Spark
Silver KL0 are the later codes, Jet Silver KG1 and Dark Blue Pearl TH1 the earlier ones), but
**it was not confirmed that the swap happened in Aug 1991 rather than Feb 1993.** Also unresolved:
Greyish Blue Pearl (BL0) is described by the code guides as a special-order R32 colour, and no
source was found saying which years or grades could order it.

https://gazoo.com/catalog/maker/NISSAN/SKYLINE/199308/1501403/ ,

| Super Clear Red (PM) | スーパークリアレッド（ＰＭ） | not found | bright red pearl metallic; **factory option only** | no |

**Feb 1997 to Jan 1999 V-spec:** the same six standards, except Spark Silver becomes **Sonic Silver
(M)** (KR4, a slightly darker and cooler metallic silver) and Dark Grey Pearl gains an (M) suffix;
the red option becomes **Super Clear Red II**. Every colour is additionally offered with the Super
Fine Hard Coat finish as a separate option line.

**Midnight Purple is not a V-spec-only colour on the R33.** The plain Jan 1995 GT-R carries the
identical seven-colour list, Midnight Purple included and equally standard. On this generation the
grade makes no difference to the palette at all.

**And LP2 is not GT-R-exclusive.** The same colour ran on the 180SX, the S14 Silvia and the Z32
Fairlady Z in the same period. Its GT-R association is cultural rather than factual.

**Confidence: solid** on names and dates, from three dated grade catalogues. Partial on codes: only
LP2 is directly attested, the rest are cross-referenced from other Nissans of the period.

**Gap:** no shade source was found for Dark Grey Pearl beyond its family heading. The 1996 LM
Limited's Championship Blue is a separate limited grade and is **not** part of the V-spec list.

## 81. Subaru Impreza 22B-STi, 1998

**One colour. There is nothing to choose.**

## What this pass established about method

Three things worth carrying forward beyond the thirteen cars.

**Gazoo's colour-family headings are the single most productive source of shade evidence in this
whole exercise**, and they paid out repeatedly here: Athlete Silver under PURPLE, Montego Blue Mica
under GREEN, Nurburgring Blue under CYAN against Royal Navy Blue under TEAL, Greyish Green Mica
under GREY, New Imola Orange under YELLOW, Medium Purplish Grey under GREY. In every one of those
cases the heading says something the name does not.

**Grade pages of the same months are each other's control.** Pulling both the base grade and the
Type V of the S2000, or both the plain GT-R and the V-spec of the R33, caught a standard/option
discrepancy and confirmed a shared list respectively. Where only one page was pulled, the
confidence rating says so.

**A colour name is not portable across a model's life.** Every multi-year car here changed its list
at least once inside its own roster window, and two of them (the AE86, the GC8) kept a name while
changing what was under it. Any authoring that treats a car as having one palette will be wrong.

## 83. Nissan Skyline GT-R V-Spec II (BNR34), 2000-2002

The roster window matches the V-spec II grade exactly: Oct 2000 to Aug 2002. Gazoo's catalogue for
that grade lists six, all manufacturer standard, all in the Super Fine Hard Coat finish.

| name | Japanese | code | shade | iconic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bayside Blue (M) | ベイサイドブルー（Ｍ） | TV2 | a deep, saturated mid-blue metallic that reads as a bright steel blue in direct light and a much darker night blue in shade. It is a warm blue rather than a cold grey-blue, and it is neither navy nor pastel. Filed under BLUE | **yes, the R34's signature** |
| Athlete Silver (M) | アスリートシルバー（Ｍ） | KV2 | silver with a clear violet cast. **Gazoo files it under PURPLE, not silver, on two separate grade pages**, which is the strongest available evidence that this is a purple-tinted silver rather than a neutral one | no |
| Black Pearl | ブラックパール | GV1 | black with a fine pearl | no |
| Sparkling Silver (M) | スパークリングシルバー（Ｍ） | WV2 | bright metallic silver with fine sparkle, neutral | no |
| White Pearl (3P) | ホワイトパール（３Ｐ） | QX1 | three-stage pearl white with a warm glow | no |
| White | ホワイト | QM1 | plain solid white; the single most common R34 GT-R colour at 25.8 per cent of production | no |

**On the plain GT-R of the same date, only White is standard and the other five are paid options.**
The V-spec II is the grade where the whole palette comes as standard. Same six colours, different
money, which is a real difference if a pool is weighted by what people actually bought.

**Bayside Blue is R34-only among these three Skylines.** It appears on the R34 Skyline from the May
1998 launch of the range and on the GT-R from Jan 1999, and runs to the end in Aug 2002. It is not
an R32 or an R33 colour, and putting it on either would be wrong. It is the most common R34 GT-R
colour after plain White, at 24.9 per cent of production.

**Midnight Purple on the R34 is two different colours, and neither is in the standard list above.**

| name | code | shade | built | iconic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Purple II | LV4 | a deep rich purple that shifts with viewing angle: green in direct sun, purple through the mid-angles, and a bronze or red-orange cast at the edges of a panel. Distinctly more aggressive than the R33's LP2 | 347 (282 V-spec, 65 GT-R) | **yes** |
| Midnight Purple III | LX0 | lighter and noticeably bluer than LV4, a multi-layer paint carrying a fine gold-bronze flake; reads as a deep violet or a rich blue-purple with gold highlights depending on where the light falls | 199 (133 V-spec, 66 GT-R) | **yes, the rarest of the three** |

**Timing, which matters for pool authoring.** LV4 arrived with the Jan 1999 car, was announced as a
run of 300, and is associated with the first V-spec. LX0 is the later of the two. **Its start date
is the one thing here that did not resolve:** a Japanese history of the colour puts it at January
2000, English aggregators at 2001-2002, and Gran Turismo catalogues its car as a '00. The
133-of-199 V-spec split is consistent with an early-2000 start, while the first V-spec was still on
sale, rather than a 2001 one. Both are limited-run factory colours, absent from the standard grade
catalogue, and together they are 4.7 per cent of all R34 GT-R production.

**Neither Midnight Purple is GT-R-exclusive as a colour.** LP2 also ran on the 180SX, the S14
Silvia and the Z32; LV4 on the Silvia and the Stagea; LX0 on the Stagea and later the 350Z. The
association with the GT-R is cultural, not factual, and a pool that treats purple as a GT-R-only
signal will be wrong about the rest of the roster.

Also present in the window but on **other grades**, not the V-spec II: Millennium Jade (JW0, 300
built, the Feb 2002 Nür cars) and Silica Brass (EY0, 131 built, the M-spec). Active Red (AR2),
Sonic Silver (KR4) and Lightning Yellow (EV1) belong to the Jan 1999 to Aug 2000 V-spec list and
were dropped at the V-spec II.

**Confidence: solid** on the standard six from a dated grade catalogue, cross-checked against a
production registry that accounts for all 14 R34 GT-R colours by code and count.

**Gap:** the LX0 order window, as set out above. **Do not state an LX0 start month without
resolving it.** Athlete Silver's purple cast rests on gazoo's family heading alone, seen on two
separate grade pages but with no photographic or descriptive source found to confirm it.
